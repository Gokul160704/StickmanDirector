## EPISODE STRUCTURE

For each segment, record Time, Purpose, complete Narration, full-frame 16:9 Still Visuals, Evidence claim IDs, and Music and sound. Use these parts in order:

1. Opening hook
2. Subject introduction
3. Background/context

## FULL NARRATION

Label each passage `FACT`, `ANALYSIS`, or `OPINION`. FACT passages require verified claim IDs; never invent interviews, witness accounts, quotations, statistics, or visual evidence.
